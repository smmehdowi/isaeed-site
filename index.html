iS iSaeed

A creator tool for uploading original videos to TikTok drafts at full quality.

Upload once. Keep the quality.

iSaeed sends your original video file directly to TikTok's official Content Posting API, so the video is never re-encoded by a mobile editor. Your file is delivered to your own TikTok inbox as a draft — you review it, add a sound if you like, and tap Post.

iSaeed is a personal, single-user tool operated by its developer. It connects only to the TikTok account that authorizes it.

How it works

  • You sign in with TikTok and grant the upload permission.
  • You select a video file from your device.
  • The file is transferred to TikTok as a draft, at its original quality.
  • You finish the post inside the TikTok app.
