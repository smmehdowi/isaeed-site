Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what data iSaeed ("the Service") handles and how it is protected. The Service is a personal, single-user tool operated by its developer.

1. Data we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens — issued when you connect your TikTok account. Stored encrypted at rest and used only to call TikTok's official API on your behalf.
  • Basic TikTok profile — your display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Video files you choose to upload — transmitted to TikTok's Content Posting API and kept in temporary storage on the server until the upload is finished or you delete it.
  • Basic technical data — upload status and timestamps kept in a local database for your upload history.

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to deliver your selected videos to your own TikTok drafts at your request and to display your upload history. The Service performs no analytics, profiling, or advertising.

3. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok through its official Content Posting API, as required to deliver your video. Your data is never sold or shared with any other third party.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to the Service requires a password, and the server is protected with HTTPS. Video files are removed from temporary storage after processing or when you delete them.

5. Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept until you disconnect your TikTok account. Upload history and video files can be deleted at any time, and you may request full deletion of all stored data by email.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for use by them.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
